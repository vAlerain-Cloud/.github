# MAGR — Magnus Algorithmic Research Group

**MAGR** is an independent research group for theoretical algorithms, data structures, and computational models. 🧠

We explore exact and approximate methods for dynamic problems, geometric aggregation, linear-algebraic representations, and high-performance algorithmic systems. Our work includes research notes, prototypes, and open-source implementations related to dynamic data structures, spectral approximation, computational geometry, and chess engine development.

Our goal is simple: define meaningful problems, build precise abstractions, and turn strong ideas into rigorous research artifacts. ✨
